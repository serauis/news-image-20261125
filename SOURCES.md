## Date rule used

A story counts if the event happened or was FIRST REPORTED on 25 November
2026 **in some time zone**. The competition's own rule. In practice:

- Items published on 25 Nov UTC: clearly in.
- Items first reported after 13:00 UTC on 24 Nov: already 25 Nov in
  Asia–Pacific (UTC+11/+13, e.g. Sydney, Auckland) — in.
- Items reported earlier on 24 Nov, or on 22–23 Nov, were EXCLUDED, even
  when they were big: COP31's collapse (22 Nov), the ICJ Myanmar/Rohingya
  ruling (19 Nov), Nvidia's +31.5% Monday rally (23 Nov), the South Korea
  Russian-jets alert (24 Nov morning Korea time). COP31 appears only as a
  25-Nov-Asia *reactions* item.
